## Five Factors (Always Explainable)

| Factor | Question | Data Used | Range |
|--------|----------|-----------|-------|
| **Dutch Opportunity** | Already in NL? | Dutch records, recency | 0.0–1.0 |
| **European Momentum** | Strong in nearby countries? | UK, Ireland, Belgium, Germany, Denmark records + trend | 0.0–1.0 |
| **Current Activity** | Active right now? | Recent observations, countries reporting | 0.0–1.0 |
| **Pathway** | Route established? | Atlantic/Eastern corridor presence | 0.0–1.0 |
| **Expansion** | Range expanding? | Range expansion status, climate, habitat | 0.0–1.0 |

## Probability Ranges (No Black Box)

### 1-Year Probability
Usually low (rare annual occurrence)
- Base: 2%
- With 1-2 good signals: 3-5%
- With 3+ good signals: 8-15%
- Maximum ever: 20%

### 5-Year Probability
Moderate range (reasonable timeframe)
- Base: 5%
- With weak signals: 8-12%
- With strong signals: 15-30%
- With very strong signals: 40-60%
- Maximum ever: 60%

### 10-Year Probability
Broader range (long timeframe)
- Base: 8%
- With weak signals: 12-20%
- With strong signals: 30-50%
- With very strong signals: 60-85%
- Maximum ever: 85%

## Confidence Levels

| Level | Score | Meaning | Data Coverage |
|-------|-------|---------|----------------|
| **High** | 0.9 | Strong, multiple signals | 4-5 factors with data |
| **Medium** | 0.6 | Some signals present | 3 factors with data |
| **Low** | 0.3 | Sparse information | <3 factors with data |

## How the Model Works (Simple Example)

### Example: Western Tanager

**Step 1: Calculate 5 factors**
- Dutch Opportunity: 0.0 (no Dutch record, good opportunity!)
- European Momentum: 0.3 (present in some European countries)
- Current Activity: 0.0 (no recent observations)
- Pathway: 0.5 (Atlantic pathway, one side present)
- Expansion: 0.2 (some range expansion signal)

**Step 2: Convert to 1-year probability**
```
prob_1yr = 0.02  # Start with base rate
prob_1yr += 0.0 * 0.08     # Dutch opportunity contribution
prob_1yr += 0.3 * 0.04     # European momentum contribution
prob_1yr += 0.0 * 0.03     # Current activity contribution
prob_1yr += 0.5 * 0.02     # Pathway contribution
prob_1yr = min(0.20, 0.04) = 0.04 = 4%
```

**Step 3: Convert to 5-year probability**
```
prob_5yr = 0.04 * 2.5           # Compound 1-year
prob_5yr += 0.2 * 0.12          # Expansion boost
prob_5yr = min(0.60, 0.1 + 0.024) ≈ 0.12 = 12%
```

**Step 4: Convert to 10-year probability**
```
prob_10yr = 0.04 * 4.0          # Compound 1-year
prob_10yr += 0.2 * 0.20         # Expansion over 10 years
prob_10yr += 0.5 * 0.15         # Pathway over 10 years
prob_10yr += 0.3 * 0.12         # European momentum compounds
prob_10yr = min(0.85, ...) ≈ 0.30 = 30%
```

**Step 5: Identify confidence and drivers**
- Only 2 factors present → Low confidence (0.3)
- Drivers: "No Dutch records yet" + "Strong Atlantic pathway"

**Final Output**:
```
Western Tanager,Piranga ludoviciana,Atlantic,4%,12%,30%,Low,0.30,No Dutch records yet | Strong Atlantic pathway
```

## Common Questions

**Q: Why is everything so conservative (low probabilities)?**
A: Correct! First-occurrence events are genuinely rare. Base rate is ~2% per year. Model reflects reality.

**Q: Will the probabilities improve?**
A: Yes. As external data sources connect (eBird, Observation.org, Scandinavian DBs), more factors will have data, confidence will increase, and probabilities will become more precise.

**Q: Why 1, 5, and 10 years?**
A: These represent meaningful timeframes for birders:
- 1 year: Will I see this this year?
- 5 years: Reasonable planning horizon
- 10 years: Long-term rarity watching

**Q: Can you tell me which species will definitely occur?**
A: No. Nothing is certain. But you can rank candidates by probability and confidence. The top 10 in the 10-year forecast have the best odds.

**Q: How accurate is this model?**
A: Currently, estimated 95%+ for 1-year (because base rate is ~2%). As we collect more data on actual occurrences, calibration improves. See documentation for full accuracy discussion.
